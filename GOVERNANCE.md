# 8x8 Public Governance

## Authority

FlashTM8 is the current owner and final authority for private 8x8 infrastructure, credentials, financial capabilities and production promotion. Public contribution does not grant access to those systems.

Repository maintainers may review, test and merge public-safe contributions within their assigned repository permissions. No automated reviewer, agent, plugin, vote or popularity score can independently widen authority.

## Evidence-gated releases

8x8 uses bounded release units. Each unit must declare its exact scope and progress through evidence states such as:

`CLAIMED → DESIGNED → IMPLEMENTED → TESTED → RECEIPT_VERIFIED → DEPLOYED → PUBLICLY_RELEASED`

A unit may reach 100/100 only for its declared scope. That score never implies completion of unrelated areas.

Public or production promotion requires:

1. exact source commit and artifact identity;
2. current tests and security/privacy/accessibility checks;
3. resolved required reviews;
4. dependency and license validation;
5. tested rollback;
6. exact deployment target;
7. a deployment receipt;
8. owner approval or an exact pre-authorized standing mandate where applicable.

## Community proposals and voting

Issues, discussions, ratings and future token-based signals may help prioritize work. They are advisory and cannot authorize credential access, spending, trading, signing, private-data exposure or production changes.

## Upstream projects

8x8 may integrate lawful forks, adapters, APIs, plugins and clean-room implementations. Upstream ownership, licenses and notices remain intact. The `8x8-compatible` label describes conformance, not ownership transfer.

## Changes to governance

Material governance changes require a public pull request, review evidence and explicit owner acceptance. Security-sensitive details may be documented privately while the public decision and its scope remain visible.
