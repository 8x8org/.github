## Completion contract

No item is complete because a document, issue, branch, process, database row, heartbeat, screenshot, or model response exists.

A bounded item is complete only when:

1. scope and acceptance criteria are finite;
2. owner and authority are known;
3. implementation exists at an exact digest;
4. tests pass at that digest;
5. security, privacy, provenance, accessibility, compatibility, and resource gates pass where applicable;
6. deployment or runtime state is independently verified when claimed;
7. cleanup and rollback are proven;
8. contradictions and residual blockers are recorded;
9. reality and evidence states are correct;
10. a receipt is emitted.

The system can achieve `100/100` for a declared release unit. The complete living 8x8 estate remains continuously maintained rather than permanently finished.
