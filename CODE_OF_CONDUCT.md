# 8x8 Community Code of Conduct

The 8x8 public community is committed to technically rigorous, respectful and safe collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give clear, actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, consent, cultural differences, accessibility needs and legal rights.
- Credit upstream authors and contributors.
- Keep security reports and personal information in appropriate private channels.
- Accept evidence-backed correction when a claim, test or implementation is wrong.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or sexualized conduct.
- Publishing private information, credentials, wallet material or precise location without authorization.
- Deceptive claims about deployment, performance, ownership, profit or security.
- Plagiarism, license removal or presenting upstream work as exclusively owned by 8x8.
- Using project infrastructure for malware, unauthorized access, market manipulation or other abuse.
- Pressuring maintainers to bypass safety, review or owner-approval gates.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content; close contributions; restrict participation; or report conduct to the relevant platform when necessary. Decisions should be proportional, documented where safe, and focused on protecting contributors and users.

Report sensitive conduct privately using the route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not place confidential details in a public issue.
