## Highest-value combined ideas

1. **Evidence-native observability:** mission traces combining Langfuse, Logfire, LangWatch, MLflow and VoltAgent presentation patterns.
2. **Typed SDK and plugin contract:** OpenAI Agents, Pydantic AI, Semantic Kernel and Vercel AI patterns translated into deny-by-default 8x8 capabilities.
3. **Visual mission designer:** Flowise and Activepieces usability with 8x8 leases, resource ceilings, receipts and owner gates.
4. **Knowledge ingestion plane:** LlamaIndex-style connectors with immutable provenance, retention, deletion and freshness metadata.
5. **Curated integration registry:** LangChain-scale ecosystem discoverability without automatically accepting its dependency or authority sprawl.
6. **Realtime voice and multimodal lane:** LiveKit Agents and TEN Framework patterns under explicit consent and session controls.
7. **Developer-first presentation:** Mastra, Pydantic, Charmbracelet and Microsoft education repositories as references for hero, quick start, examples, diagrams and navigation.
8. **Evaluation and release qualification:** RagaAI, LangWatch and MLflow patterns for scenario suites, regression comparison and promotion gates.
