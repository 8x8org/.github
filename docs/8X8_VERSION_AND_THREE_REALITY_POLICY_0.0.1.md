# 8x8 Version and Three-Reality Policy 0.0.1

**Owner:** FlashTM8 ⚡️ / Meher Trabelsi  
**Status:** Canonical beta policy  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-06

## Canonical version

The complete 8x8 system remains at **0.0.1**.

This version applies across public repositories, private repositories, architecture documents, schemas, manifests, interfaces, release notes, screenshots, demonstrations, agents, bodies, missions, protocols, products, services and research artifacts unless a lower-level dependency must preserve its upstream version for technical or legal accuracy.

`0.0.1` means:

- the system is early beta and under active convergence;
- no whole-system 100/100 or fully stable claim is authorized;
- public features may be usable while remaining incomplete;
- private runtime components may be operational without being public or production-grade;
- Future work remains research, proposal, simulation or isolated prototype until promoted by evidence;
- version numbers such as V1, V2, v14 or release-unit numbers are historical identifiers or internal implementation generations, not the product maturity version.

## Required display form

Use these forms where appropriate:

- `8x8 OS 0.0.1`
- `8x8 User Edition 0.0.1 Beta`
- `Protocol 0.0.1`
- `Schema 0.0.1`
- `Architecture 0.0.1`
- `Research Snapshot 0.0.1`

Do not use `1.0`, `stable`, `production complete`, `100/100`, `fully autonomous`, or equivalent language without a later explicit owner-approved promotion backed by exact evidence.

## The Three Realities are executable system state

Past, Present and Future are not slogans. They are native 8x8 realities that classify every meaningful object and control how it may move.

### 1. PRIVATE_PAST

The complete private history and operational memory of 8x8, including prior missions, receipts, local runtime state, superseded implementations, archived decisions, private repositories, recovery evidence and owner-only context.

Past does not mean dead or irrelevant. It is the evidence-bearing historical substrate from which the system learns, restores and proves continuity.

### 2. PUBLIC_PRESENT

The currently approved, privacy-safe and evidence-backed public projection of 8x8. Only capabilities with current tests, provenance, security review, accessibility review, approved claims and rollback may appear here.

Present does not mean complete. At version 0.0.1 it is a truthful beta surface.

### 3. FUTURE_LAB

Research, candidate repositories, designs, simulations, experiments, isolated prototypes, proposed agents, worlds, models, economic mechanisms and unpromoted capabilities.

Future does not mean promised or implemented. Nothing in Future Lab may be represented as publicly available merely because it has a document, branch, mockup or impressive screenshot.

## Protected beta as a transition state

`PROTECTED_BETA` is a controlled transition between Future Lab and Public Present. It is not a fourth reality. It is a promotion gate where selected users, test accounts or isolated environments validate a candidate under limited authority.

## Every object must declare reality

The following objects must carry a reality classification:

- repositories and branches;
- agents and bodies;
- services and nodes;
- missions and projects;
- features and products;
- models, providers and connectors;
- visual assets and demonstrations;
- releases and badges;
- claims and documentation;
- testnet and economic artifacts;
- receipts, evidence and context claims.

Required values:

- `PRIVATE_PAST`
- `PUBLIC_PRESENT`
- `FUTURE_LAB`

Optional transition metadata:

- `promotion_state: PROTECTED_BETA`
- source reality;
- target reality;
- promotion receipt;
- owner gate;
- rollback target;
- evidence freshness.

## Promotion contract

Movement toward Public Present requires:

1. canonical object identity;
2. exact artifact digest;
3. declared source and target reality;
4. privacy review;
5. security review;
6. license and provenance review;
7. tests and acceptance evidence;
8. accessibility evidence where user-facing;
9. cleanup and rollback proof;
10. owner approval;
11. approved public claim manifest;
12. version label `0.0.1` until the system-wide version changes.

## Demotion and revocation

Public objects can return to Protected Beta or Future Lab. Claims can be revoked. Credentials can expire. Compromised artifacts can be quarantined. Superseded evidence remains in Private Past but no longer controls Present truth.

## Naming migration rule

Existing filenames, mission IDs, service generations and historical documents containing `V1`, `V2`, `v8`, `v14` or similar identifiers must not be destructively rewritten when doing so would break links, provenance, deployment references or evidence chains.

Instead:

- preserve the historical identifier;
- add `product_version: 0.0.1` or an equivalent header;
- label the historical value as `implementation_generation`, `document_revision`, `protocol_revision` or `service_generation`;
- ensure public-facing titles and badges display `0.0.1 Beta`;
- migrate filenames only through explicit redirects or supersession manifests.

This distinction prevents the rather catastrophic idea of renaming runtime generation `v14` to `0.0.1` and then wondering why service paths, tests and receipts no longer resolve.

## Completion language

Allowed:

- implemented in a bounded scope;
- verified at an exact commit;
- deployed on a named node;
- available in protected beta;
- public in 0.0.1 beta;
- planned for Future Lab;
- blocked pending evidence or owner approval.

Not allowed without explicit proof:

- entire system complete;
- fully stable;
- 100/100 everywhere;
- production-ready across all nodes;
- every connector synchronized;
- all agents active;
- future capability already present.

## Immediate migration scope

1. organization profile and public README version banners;
2. public repository badges and release labels;
3. architecture and research document headers;
4. schemas and manifests with `product_version: 0.0.1`;
5. project fields for Reality and Evidence State;
6. agent/body passports;
7. mission and receipt schemas;
8. protected-beta and public promotion receipts;
9. private repository role documents;
10. automated validation preventing unsupported stable or 1.0 claims.

## Canonical truth

Until a future owner-approved version promotion occurs, **8x8 is version 0.0.1 across its complete system identity**. Historical revisions remain visible as subordinate implementation metadata. Past, Present and Future remain executable, evidence-governed realities inside the system, not decorative words.
